<p>DashGrid is an independent fork of AARE, the open-source replacement for the phone side of Android Auto. It adds switchable workspaces, a configurable grid of apps and widgets, and a phone-side editor, while keeping AARE's Android Auto projection, car audio, navigation, music, messages, calls and voice assistant.</p>

<p><b>Workspaces</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Any number of home screens, each with its own name and order.</li>
<li>Switch cyclically by swipe or page dots.</li>
<li>Layouts: 100 %, 50/50, 60/40, 70/30, 30/70, three widgets, four widgets, or a custom grid.</li>
<li>Live app panes draw an app into its cells; a per-workspace option opens one full screen when you tap its free space.</li>
<li>Drag shortcuts, panes and widgets, resize and merge cells, pin a map or music widget to an app.</li>
<li>Edited on the phone; the car screen stays a clean viewer.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Inherited from AARE</b></p>
<ul>
<li>USB and wireless Android Auto projection without Google Play Services.</li>
<li>Open navigation apps, any media app, messaging, calls and the steering-wheel assistant.</li>
<li>Car audio, day/night themes, split screen, OBD, Home actions, the phone-screen mirror.</li>
<li>No tracking; only the weather, certificate and update requests AARE already makes.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Fork note</b></p>
<p>DashGrid is a fork of AARE (GPL-3.0), with its own application ID, signing key, icon and settings. It installs alongside AARE. Many factory head units only accept Google's Android Auto certificate; DashGrid offers the same one-tap certificate download as AARE. Some apps trust only AARE's signing certificate and need DashGrid added to their host list.</p>
